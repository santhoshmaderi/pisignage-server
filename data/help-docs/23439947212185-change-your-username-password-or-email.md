# Change your Username, Password or Email

Source: https://help.pisignage.com/hc/en-us/articles/23439947212185-Change-your-Username-Password-or-Email

This article guides you to change the Username/ Password/ Email associated with your pisignage.com account.

 

- Login to your pisignage.com account.

- Click on the Dropdown next to your username to reveal the Profile Settings option and Click on it.

- Enter your Current Password and Click Unlock to modify your username/password/email.

- Click on any of the Pen icons  to modify the necessary field.

- Click on Save to save your changes.

 

Note: 
1) You need to re-login to your account if you change your username or password.

2) If you change your username, you also need to re-register your players by clicking on the License-icon in the Players tab
