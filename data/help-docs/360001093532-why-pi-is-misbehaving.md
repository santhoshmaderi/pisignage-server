# Why pi is misbehaving 

Source: https://help.pisignage.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001093532-Why-pi-is-misbehaving
Keywords: Lightning, TV USB

If you are powering your device from TV USB port, the power may not be sufficient to drive ethernet interface.

Use separate power adapter which powers minimum of 2A.

If the power is not sufficient Pi may misbehave, face connectivity issues and a lightning icon is seen on the top right hand side of the screen.
