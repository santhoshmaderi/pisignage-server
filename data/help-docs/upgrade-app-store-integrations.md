# App store & integrations (piSignage managed)

Source: https://pisignage.com
Keywords: app store, apps, plugin, plugins, widget, widgets, integration, integrations, marketplace, add-ons

Looking for ready-made apps, widgets and integrations (weather, social, data feeds, business tools) rather than hand-building each one? The **app store and integrations** are part of **piSignage managed**.

What you get:
- **App store** — drop-in apps and widgets you can add to playlists without coding.
- **Integrations** — connect to third-party data and services to drive on-screen content.
- **Regular additions** — new apps and integrations delivered through the managed platform.

Best for teams that want richer content sources with minimal setup.
