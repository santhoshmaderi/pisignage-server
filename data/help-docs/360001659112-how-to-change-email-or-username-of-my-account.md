# How to change email or username of my account

Source: https://help.pisignage.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001659112-How-to-change-email-or-username-of-my-account

Changing of email is not possible in the front end. But if you send us the old email, username and new email we can change the email for you. Also give us some details to make sure that you are the legitimate user of that account!

All the assets, groups and players are attached with username of the account. So it is not possible to change the username. However if you create another account with the new username, we can migrate your old account to new account.
