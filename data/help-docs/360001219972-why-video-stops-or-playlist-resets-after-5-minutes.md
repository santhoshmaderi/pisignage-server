# Why video stops or playlist resets after 5 minutes

Source: https://help.pisignage.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001219972-Why-video-stops-or-playlist-resets-after-5-minutes

If video duration is not obtained during upload, there is a default duration of 300 seconds is set. The video stops playing after 300 seconds in that case. Please upload the video again or correct the duration in the playlist.
