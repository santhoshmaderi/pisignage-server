# Storage space available per user at pisignage.com

Source: https://help.pisignage.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001240691-Storage-space-available-per-user-at-pisignage-com

Every account at pisignage.com is allocated (Number of licensed players) GB space. i.e. If you have 5 licensed players, you are allotted 5GB of space. If you need more space you can purchase in multiples of 1GB at monthly subscription.
