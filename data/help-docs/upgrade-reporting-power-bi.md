# Reporting & Power BI (piSignage managed)

Source: https://help.pisignage.com/hc/en-us/articles/58058827745049-Showing-a-Power-BI-report-on-your-screens
Keywords: power bi, powerbi, reporting, reports, analytics, dashboard, proof of play, audit trail, business intelligence

Need to show live business dashboards on your screens, or prove what actually played? That's in **piSignage managed**.

What you get:
- **Power BI on screens** — display live Power BI reports and dashboards on your players, with secure token-based access and auto-refresh.
- **Proof-of-play / audit reporting** — see which assets played, where and when, for compliance and advertiser reporting.
- **Fleet analytics** — richer reporting across all your players and groups.

Ideal for retail, corporate comms, and advertising networks that must report on what was displayed.
