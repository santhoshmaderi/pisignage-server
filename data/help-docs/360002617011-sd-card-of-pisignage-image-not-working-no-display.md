# SD card of piSignage image not working - no display

Source: https://help.pisignage.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002617011-SD-card-of-piSignage-image-not-working-no-display

piSignage image while booting only support HDMI connected TVs, if no display appears please check your display type and the cable. 

You could connect to a normal TV with HDMI, configure the /boot/config.txt for your display type as mentioned in article https://help.pisignage.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001120692-Supporting-different-display-types.
