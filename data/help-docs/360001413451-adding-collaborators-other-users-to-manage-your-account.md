# Adding collaborators (other users) to manage your account

Source: https://help.pisignage.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001413451-Adding-collaborators-other-users-to-manage-your-account

You can add more  users to manage your installation. You could specify the access rights so that they can perform only specific functions or see only groups they are assigned to.

To add a person as collaborator

- Ask the user to register at pisignage.com and note down their username(not email).

- Under your installation settings, add collaborators using their usernames under installation settings

- You can give the selective access to groups, playlists and assets by selecting the access rights from the popup screen.
