# Audio output on 3.5mm port

Source: https://help.pisignage.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001121132-Audio-output-on-3-5mm-port
Keywords: aux port

By default audio is output on both HDMI and aux audio port.

In case where there is a audio playlist is also scheduled for the player Group, audio playlist output is played on 3.5mm port. Main playlist video or audio content is played on HDMI port.

In case Analog (PAL or NTSC) video mode is selected (under Group settings), 3.5mm aux port will be used for connecting the TV.
