# White-label & branding (piSignage managed / partners)

Source: https://pisignage.com/partners
Keywords: white label, white-label, branding, custom domain, reseller, resellers, partner, partners, agency

Want to run digital signage under your own brand and domain, or resell it to your clients? That's the **white-label / partner** offering.

What you get:
- **White-label server** — the same platform as pisignage.com, customized with your logo, domain name and UI branding.
- **Custom domain** — host it on your own URL.
- **Reseller program** — bundle hardware + software, manage clients, and grow a signage business with bulk player licensing.

Ideal for agencies, integrators and MSPs offering signage as a service.
