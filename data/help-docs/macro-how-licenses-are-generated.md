# How licenses are generated

Keywords: license, register, credits, managed, subscription

Licenses are tied to the Pi. To generate licenses:
1. When you purchase at pisignage.com, your account is credited with licenses (managed, or player-only; managed adds 12 credits per player).
2. Under the Players tab, register each player with the Register button. The license is generated on the server, emailed to you, and (for managed players) downloaded to the player automatically.
3. You can view and download licenses anytime under the Subscriptions tab.
For open-source servers, the license file must be uploaded to the local server manually.
