# How to purchase licenses and subscription

Source: https://help.pisignage.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001236071-How-to-purchase-licenses-and-subscription

1. Login to pisignage.com using your credentials.

2. Goto subscriptions tab

3. You can purchase managed license ($45)  which includes player license and 1 year server subscription or just player license ($25). You can also purchase renewal subscriptions at $20.

4. Click the relevant PayPal button which takes you to PayPal page.

5. Select the quantity.

6. You can pay either using International Credit Cards or PayPal account.

7. Return to pisignage.com through PayPal url so that licenses are properly updated.

8. If there are any issues, discrepancies please write to support@pisignage.com
