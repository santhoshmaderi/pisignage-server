# How to delete a player and redeem license

Source: https://help.pisignage.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001235451-How-to-delete-a-player-and-redeem-license
Keywords: remove player, replace player, redemption

Under settings, delete the player(s) you do not want anymore. The license will be automatically credited to your account. 

New UI: Go to Settings → Screen Management. Under Manage Players, you can remove or disable the player(s) you do not want anymore.

Once the licenses are adjusted you may have to register the players which do not have license again.

In case of non-managed players, write to us at support@pisignage.com to delete the player and adjust the license.
