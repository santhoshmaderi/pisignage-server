# Support for PAL/NTSC analog RCA Video

Source: https://help.pisignage.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001149611-Support-for-PAL-NTSC-analog-RCA-Video

- piSignage player can be connected over RCA cable to PAL/NTSC TVs

- Purchase a standard 3.5mm 3pin to RCA cable and connect to TV from the 3.5mm connector of Pi

- Disconnect HDMI cable

- Under Group->Settings choose PAL or NTSC option as needed

- TV OFF/ON and other CEC functionalities are not available in this mode
