# How credits and payment-due work

Keywords: credits, payment due, subscription, renewal, billing

piSignage uses a credit system per player. Each active player consumes 1 credit per month.
- A $20 subscription adds 12 credits (one player active for a year).
- 24 credits are added free every year to cover the 2 free players included with each account.
- Credits are not tied to a specific player; any player consumes available credits.
- The "payment due" notice appears when less than 2 months of credits remain.
- Credits do not expire; new credits stack on existing ones.
Purchase under the Subscription/Licenses tab: choose $20 - 1 year renewal, set the quantity to your number of paid players, and pay via PayPal/Stripe/Razorpay.
