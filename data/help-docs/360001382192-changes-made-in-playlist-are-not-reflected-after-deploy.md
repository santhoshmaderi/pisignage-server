# Changes made in playlist are not reflected after Deploy

Source: https://help.pisignage.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001382192-Changes-made-in-playlist-are-not-reflected-after-Deploy

In some cases, changes made to the playlist are not reflected in the player after Deploy. 1.9.8a beta has this issue fixed. If your release is prior to this, please upgrade to latest.
