# On 4K resolution and 4K video support on Raspberry Pi 4

Source: https://help.pisignage.com/hc/en-us/articles/360038255892-On-4K-resolution-and-4K-video-support-on-Raspberry-Pi-4
Keywords: pi 4

piSignage supports upto 4K resolution based on TV resolution with Raspberry Pi 4.

Current standalone video players commonly available for Raspberry Pi  only support H.264 hardware decoding which is limited to 1080p video in pi GPU.

Only H.265 (HEVC) videos can be hardware decoded upto 4K resolutions in Pi GPU and currently is not supported by the standalone players available or piSignage uses.

Till we identify a suitable standalone player only videos upto 1080p are supported by piSignage in Pi 4 as well  although they are scaled to 4K.
