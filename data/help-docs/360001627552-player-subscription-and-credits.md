# Player subscription and credits

Source: https://help.pisignage.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001627552-Player-subscription-and-credits
Keywords: renew license

When you purchase a managed license you get a player license and 12 credits for subscription at pisignage.com. 

Every month the day you have signed in at pisignage.com, the subscription management module, counts the number of players that are registered (as managed) & used in that month and deducts 1 credit per player per month. 

You can see your subscriptions and remaining credits in subscriptions tab. Anytime you can purchase credits (in multiples of 12) by clicking the button "Subscriptions Renewal".
